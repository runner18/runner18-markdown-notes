### One Type of Simple Setup
```
[Solar panel]=====[Solar Charge Controller]=====[battery]======[lightbulb]
```

You can't plug the solar panel into the battery/lightbulb directly. The voltage is too high I think.

The Solar Charge Controller (SCC) takes the right amount of electricity from the solar panel to charge the battery/power the lightbulb.

Some SCCs have a spot to plug in the light bulb so the setup would look like this instead:
```
[Solar panel]=====[Solar Charge Controller]=====[battery]
							||
							||
							||
							||
						[lightbulb]
```

### Pay Attention to Voltage, Current, and Resistance
Metaphor for understanding electricity - try blowing air out of your mouth:
- If you squeeze your lungs more, the air gets more squeezed and **more air flows**
- If you close your lips more, it's harder for air to move and **less air flows**

$rate\ of\ air flow=\dfrac{how\ squeezed\ the\ air\ is}{how\ hard\ it\ is\ for\ the\ air\ to\ move}$


Electricity is the same idea:

$rate\ of\ electricity\ flow = \dfrac{how\ squeezed\ the\ electricty\ is}{how\ hard\ it\ is\ for\ electricty\ to\ move}$

aka

$current=\dfrac{voltage}{resistance}$
