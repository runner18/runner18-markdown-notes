Is this diff eq equation separable?
Can you get x's and dx on one side, and y's and dy's on the other side?

Example:
Is $xy'+y=3$ separable?

| Equation              | Description                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| $xy'+y=3$             |                                           |
| $x\dfrac{dx}{dy}=3-y$ | Write in terms of $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$        |
| $x{dx}=(3-y)dy$       | x & dx on one side<br>y & dy on the other |
