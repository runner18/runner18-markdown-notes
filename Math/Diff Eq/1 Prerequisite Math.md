Example:

### Log and Natural Log

**Log:**
$log_b(n) = x$
$b^x=n$

**Common Log:**
$log N = x$
$N = 10^x$

**Natural Log:**
$lnN=x$
$N=e^x$

Example:
$ln(y)=3x+2$
$y=e^{3x+2}$

Example:
$ln(y)=2x+ln(4)$
$e^{ln(y)}=e^{2x+ln(4)}$
$y=e^{2x}*e^{ln(4)}$
$y=e^{2x}*4$
$y=4e^{2x}$
